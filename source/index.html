Group

Hire mercenaries from Liaison Brenna in Everblock Keep (E to talk). The Cleric heals and buffs, the Warrior tanks and taunts. Click ⚙ in the group window to set stance (passive / balanced / aggressive), heal threshold, and hand them gear. Dead mercenaries can be revived for a fee (E on Brenna or the Revive button). Necromancers summon a pet and command it with the pet bar above the hotbar, P (pet window) or /pet attack | backoff | follow | guard | sit. Experience is split EQ-style.

Factions

Killing gnolls, orcs and Sandreaver bandits (and finishing quests) changes your standing with the Guards, the Hollis Rangers, the Sunward Caravan and the bandits themselves. C shows how a creature or NPC regards you; merchants charge more (or refuse) when they dislike you, and factions only attack on sight at Threateningly or worse. /faction lists your standings.

Phone Mode

Toggle on the start screen (or /phone). Left thumb: joystick to move. Drag the right side to look, tap a creature to target, pinch to zoom. Buttons: jump, attack, sit, E (loot/talk), consider, bags, spellbook, map.

Graphics

Model quality:
Low = flat-shaded models, no blob shadows, fewer particles (also /gfx low).

Tips

Con colors: green < light blue < blue < white < yellow < red. Creatures that con green or grey give little or no experience.
When you die you return to your bind point without your gear. Run back and loot your corpse (a yellow arrow points the way).
Train new spells and skills at Guildmaster Aldric as you level. The Sunken Crypt lies to the northeast. The North Pass leads to the Frostfang Reaches (levels 8-15) — walk through the shimmering zone line.
Quests: Merchant Tiloria, Guard Halric, Guard Mossen, Scout Hollis and Trapper Gunnar (Frostfang). Epic chain: The Warden's Legacy begins with Soulbinder Kerra.
